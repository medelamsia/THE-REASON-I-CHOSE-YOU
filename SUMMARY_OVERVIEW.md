SUMMARY / OVERVIEW

## Final adaptation model
42-episode director cut of the source.

## Hard runtime
Every episode is 60 seconds or less.

## Storyboard partition
Every episode is split into six editorial chunks: 00:00–00:10, 00:10–00:20, 00:20–00:30, 00:30–00:40, 00:40–00:50, 00:50–01:00.

These chunks exist to make downstream storyboard/video generation easier. They are not six independent scenes. Story action, blocking, emotion, wardrobe, props, environment and character knowledge continue across each boundary.

## Pacing
Do not slow the story to fill a chunk. Use rapid cuts inside a chunk where the source moves quickly. Do not add filler dialogue or invented events.

## Story architecture
The source has 49 numbered chapters plus an Epilogue in the visible table of contents. This cut regroups them into 42 publishable micro-drama episodes. Short chapters are combined; dense chapters are split where necessary.

## Director priority
1. Source canon
2. Continuity
3. Causal dramatic flow
4. Character performance
5. Pacing
6. Visual style

Episode titles are optional. Story continuation is mandatory.
