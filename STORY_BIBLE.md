# STORY BIBLE — MICRO DRAMA

## SERIES FORMAT
42 publishable episodes.
Hard maximum: 60 seconds per episode.
Each episode is divided into six 10-second production partitions.
The six partitions are continuous pieces of the same dramatic flow; they are not standalone scenes.

## DIRECTOR RULE
A source chapter is not automatically an episode.
The director combines or splits chapters according to causal flow, emotional progression, and the 60-second ceiling.
The story must never slow just because it crossed a part boundary.
Episode titles are optional; continuity is mandatory.

## DRAMATIC SPINE
### 1 — THE CHOICE
Family debt threatens the Wallsmans. Tory meets Zion without knowing him. Chase links them. Zion sees Tory again at the engagement meeting and chooses her instead of Amelia.

### 2 — THE CONTRACT
The wedding happens without romance. Zion is still tied to Nataly. The honeymoon creates jealousy, possessiveness, physical tension, and rules about intimacy and children.

### 3 — THE CRACKS
Tory insists on a career. Grandma forces public affection. Zion interferes with her job prospects because he wants her close. Nataly enters the same professional orbit.

### 4 — REAL LOVE
Éclaire becomes their shared environment. Jealousy becomes mutual. Tory's virginity makes Zion choose restraint. Nataly's exposure of the brunette bet hurts Tory. Zion answers by confessing genuine love.

### 5 — FAMILY WOUNDS
Zion's scars and childhood abuse explain his fear of fatherhood. Gerald is not just an antagonist; he is the model Zion fears becoming. Amelia's pregnancy and Angelo's birth make the children question unavoidable.

### 6 — PREGNANCY
Tory becomes pregnant, chooses to keep the baby, and hides the pregnancy because she fears Zion's reaction. Zion discovers the secret and initially rejects fatherhood.

### 7 — ACCEPTANCE
A week of distance turns into care. Zion begins accepting responsibility. Nana dies. Tory suffers a pregnancy complication, but the baby's heartbeat remains strong. Zion becomes her caretaker and the couple rebuilds.

### 8 — FINAL RUPTURE
Nataly's messages about their past pregnancy are misunderstood. Tory leaves. Zion explains the truth and gives her space. The house and nursery reveal the future he prepared.

### 9 — RESOLUTION
Gerald is finally exposed through Katherine's evidence. Tory and Zion rebuild communication and choose the marriage freely. The pregnancy reaches labor.

### 10 — EPILOGUE
Three years later Tory is professionally successful, Abby is their daughter, and Zion is fully comfortable as a father. A new pregnancy becomes a twins announcement. Abby's fear of losing her parents is resolved through the idea that love expands.

## EPISODE DETAIL STANDARD
Each episode file must contain:
- Dramatic purpose
- Beginning state
- Six continuous story beats
- Action/blocking
- Dialogue
- Performance/subtext
- Visual/edit intent
- Continuity
- Ending state
- Next-episode carry-over

## COMPRESSION STANDARD
Keep causal events and emotional turns.
Cut repetition, decorative movement, redundant reactions, and filler.
Never invent a new event to make an episode or a 10-second partition feel full.

## CONTINUITY STANDARD
Every episode passes forward:
- what Tory knows;
- what Zion knows;
- what each believes;
- relationship status;
- unresolved conflict;
- family circumstances;
- pregnancy timeline;
- current setting/wardrobe/props when relevant.

## TONE
Rom-com banter, forced-marriage tension, jealousy, family drama, trauma/recovery, pregnancy stakes, and sincere romance coexist.
Do not flatten the work into one genre.

## FINAL DIRECTOR PRINCIPLE
The goal is not to create 42 disconnected viral clips.
The goal is to create one continuous drama that happens to be published in 42 episodes.
