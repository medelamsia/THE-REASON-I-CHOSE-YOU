# EPISODE 03 — CHAPTER 3

## DIRECTOR BRIEF
**Runtime:** hard max 60s. Target ~52–58s.
**Story function:** establish the engagement day from both POVs; make Gerald's pressure concrete; show Amelia/Tory's sister bond before the choice.
## PART 1 — HAZEL
**Suggested duration:** 8s
**Story beat:** Chase introduces Tory to Zion as Hazel; both adults freeze.
**Action/blocking:** Chase waves them together, then Zion takes Chase back to the car because neither adult can form words.
**Dialogue:** “Uncle, meet my friend, Hazel.”
**Performance:** Awkward silence is the joke; don't stretch it.

## PART 2 — ENGAGEMENT DAY
**Suggested duration:** 8s
**Story beat:** Amelia reveals this is her engagement day while Tory remains unimpressed by the romance talk.
**Action/blocking:** Amelia calls Tory a little antisocial germ, takes her shopping, and prepares for the party.
**Dialogue:** “Let's go shopping, you little antisocial germ.” / “Actually, today was her engagement day.”
**Performance:** Sisterly teasing with an affectionate undercurrent.

## PART 3 — GERALD'S DEMAND
**Suggested duration:** 11s
**Story beat:** Zion returns to the mansion and openly rejects the arranged marriage.
**Action/blocking:** Gerald confronts him. Zion says he wants Nataly. Gerald insists the Wallsmans owe them money and Zion must secure succession.
**Dialogue:** “I won't do it.” / “I want to marry Nataly.” / “They owe us money, son.”
**Performance:** Zion's anger is contained until Gerald pushes the succession threat.
**Director:** This is exposition, so keep it visually active: movement through the mansion, jacket removed, car keys, interruption by Chase.

## PART 4 — SISTERS BEFORE THE STORM
**Suggested duration:** 9s
**Story beat:** Tory helps Amelia get ready and reminds her how much she worries about her sister.
**Action/blocking:** Tory complains about the dress; then she pulls Amelia toward the mirror and compliments her. The conversation turns to their mother and Amelia's blonde hair.
**Dialogue:** “Why can't I just wear trousers?” / “You look absolutely stunning.”
**Performance:** Tory becomes sincere; the humor drops briefly.
**Director:** This is the emotional foundation for the next episode's betrayal without exaggerating it.

## PART 5 — WAITING
**Suggested duration:** 6–8s
**Story beat:** The family waits for the groom.
**Action/blocking:** Amelia nervously paces; Tory eats an apple and jokes to herself about the entire situation.
**Dialogue:** Keep dialogue minimal; use ambient family chatter.
**Performance:** Tory's casual eating is a character statement.

## PART 6 — DOOR / CUT
**Suggested duration:** 9–10s
**Story beat:** The Davit family is about to arrive.
**Action/blocking:** Tory and Amelia remain upstairs while the family gathers downstairs. The incoming arrival is the visual cliffhanger.
**Dialogue:** No added line beyond source-supported exchanges.
**End/carry-over:** EP04 opens with the Davits arriving and Tory realizing who Zion really is.

## CONTINUITY LOCK
- Amelia is Tory's older sister and intended bride.
- Zion still wants Nataly.
- Gerald frames the marriage as necessary because of debt and succession.
- Tory is already protective of Amelia.
- Tory and Zion have seen each other twice but still lack a formal relationship.
