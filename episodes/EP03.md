# EP03 — ENGAGEMENT DAY

## DIRECTOR BRIEF
**Dramatic Purpose:** Carry directly from Zion's refusal into the engagement day, then make the bride switch the irreversible story event.
**Runtime:** HARD MAXIMUM 60 seconds.
**Part Timing:** six continuous 10-second production splits.
**Part Rule:** Continuous story flow. No repeated Hazel/school introduction from EP02.

## PART 01 — 00:00–00:10
**Story Beat:** AMELIA PREPARES
**Action:** Amelia and Tory prepare for the engagement. Tory resists formal clothing while Amelia is excited.
**Blocking:** Bedroom/wardrobe area; sisters work around each other.
**Dialogue:** “Why can't I just wear trousers?”
**Character Expression:** Tory sarcastic; Amelia hopeful.
**Subtext:** Tory wants Amelia happy even though she dislikes the whole marriage arrangement.
**Environment:** Wallsmans' home.
**Props:** Dress, makeup, bags.
**Continuity:** Amelia remains the expected bride.

## PART 02 — 00:10–00:20
**Story Beat:** SISTER BOND
**Action:** Tory helps Amelia get ready and compliments her while thinking about their mother and how different the sisters are.
**Blocking:** Mirror/bedroom; Tory adjusts Amelia's look.
**Dialogue:** “You look absolutely stunning.”
**Character Expression:** Warm, sincere.
**Subtext:** Tory is protecting Amelia emotionally.
**Environment:** Bedroom/mirror.
**Props:** Dress, makeup.
**Continuity:** Their bond makes the later bride switch hurt more.

## PART 03 — 00:20–00:30
**Story Beat:** ZION AT THE MANSION
**Action:** Zion arrives after picking up Chase and is confronted by Gerald about the engagement.
**Blocking:** Entry → study; Gerald intercepts him.
**Dialogue:** “I won't do it.” / “I want to marry Nataly.”
**Character Expression:** Zion angry; Gerald domineering.
**Subtext:** Zion still believes he can refuse the family contract.
**Environment:** Davit mansion.
**Props:** Keys, phone, suit jacket.
**Continuity:** The family expects the engagement to proceed despite Zion's refusal.

## PART 04 — 00:30–00:40
**Story Beat:** DAVITS ARRIVE
**Action:** The Davits reach the Wallsmans' house while Zion has not arrived with them. Tory is pulled into serving.
**Blocking:** Foyer/dining room; Tory moves between guests and the table.
**Dialogue:** “Can you help me serve?”
**Character Expression:** Tory polite outside, nervous inside.
**Subtext:** She is helping Amelia survive the awkward ceremony.
**Environment:** Wallsmans' home.
**Props:** Trays, plates, glasses.
**Continuity:** Chase and Brenda recognize Tory as Hazel.

## PART 05 — 00:40–00:50
**Story Beat:** ZION CHOOSES TORY
**Action:** Zion finally arrives, sees Tory, pulls Gerald aside and says he wants the younger sister. Gerald objects to her age but accepts the change.
**Blocking:** Zion enters → locks eyes with Tory → private hallway/side room with Gerald.
**Dialogue:** “I want the younger one.” / “She's twenty-two.”
**Character Expression:** Zion decisive; Gerald furious; Tory confused.
**Subtext:** Zion's attraction overrides the original arrangement.
**Environment:** Wallsmans' home.
**Props:** Suit jacket, phone.
**Continuity:** The choice is now irreversible.

## PART 06 — 00:50–01:00
**Story Beat:** PUBLIC REJECTION
**Action:** Zion announces he will marry Tory. Amelia breaks down and leaves; Tory follows her, then returns to confront Zion.
**Blocking:** Dining room → hallway; Tory stands face-to-face with Zion only after Amelia leaves.
**Dialogue:** “But I will marry you.” / “Fuck you.”
**Character Expression:** Amelia devastated; Tory furious; Zion resolute.
**Subtext:** Tory's anger is about Amelia being hurt and her own autonomy being taken.
**Environment:** Dining room/hallway.
**Props:** None required.
**Continuity:** EP04 starts with the consequences and wedding preparation.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Intercut faces at the announcement, then hard cut on Tory's insult.
