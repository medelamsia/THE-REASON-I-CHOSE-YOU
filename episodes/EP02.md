# EPISODE 02 — CHAPTER 2

## DIRECTOR BRIEF
**Runtime:** hard max 60s. Target ~50–55s.
**Story function:** replay the first meeting from Zion's POV, establish Gerald's marriage pressure, then connect Tory to Chase and reveal Zion is the uncle.
**Continuity:** begins with Zion immediately after fighting Gerald; ends on their second eye-contact encounter.

## PART 1 — AFTER GERALD
**Suggested duration:** 9s
**Story beat:** Zion storms out after another fight with Gerald about the marriage.
**Action/blocking:** Zion exits the mansion fast, jaw tight, gets into his car, and deliberately chooses coffee instead of vodka because it is too early.
**Dialogue:** “He was forcing me to marry someone I didn't want.” / “I decided to go for a cup of coffee since it was too early for vodka.”
**Performance:** Irritated, controlled, proud.
**Director:** Do not make him look heartbroken; his anger is the dominant state.

## PART 2 — THE GIRL
**Suggested duration:** 9s
**Story beat:** Zion waits at the café and notices the short brunette approaching his table.
**Action/blocking:** He scans the cheap café, becomes impatient, then stands when Tory reaches him. His attention locks on her before he understands why.
**Dialogue:** “Sir.”
**Performance:** Zion hears her low voice and is visibly caught off guard.
**Director:** Mirror the visual language from EP01, but from his POV. Same event, different emotional meaning.

## PART 3 — ESCAPE
**Suggested duration:** 8s
**Story beat:** Zion deliberately walks away because the encounter is affecting him.
**Action/blocking:** He storms out. Tory follows. His long strides create distance; her smaller footsteps keep coming.
**Dialogue:** “Sir.” / “Eenh... enhrm.”
**Performance:** Zion avoids looking back; Tory is simply trying to finish the customer interaction.
**Director:** Keep the chase short so it feels like a mirrored callback, not repeated content.

## PART 4 — THE EYES
**Suggested duration:** 9s
**Story beat:** Zion removes his sunglasses and studies Tory while she is distracted by his car.
**Action/blocking:** He reaches the Audi, turns, removes his glasses, follows her gaze, and watches the wind move her hair.
**Dialogue:** Tory: “Yeah, sir, your coffee. You didn't...”
**Performance:** Zion's internal attraction begins when he notices her natural brunette appearance.
**Director:** Match cut with EP01's eye reveal, then move quickly to Tory leaving.

## PART 5 — SCHOOL / CHASE
**Suggested duration:** 9s
**Story beat:** Tory goes to Amelia's school and reunites with Chase.
**Action/blocking:** Tory arrives, sees Zion's car, doubts herself, then hugs Chase enthusiastically. Chase tells her the uncle who used to live far away is back and is getting married.
**Dialogue:** “Oooh, I missed you so much.” / “I missed you too. Look, I grew a tooth!” / “He came. Now he's living here forever. I heard he's getting married.”
**Performance:** Warm, playful, immediately different from Tory's guarded behavior with Zion.
**Director:** This section establishes Chase as the bridge between their worlds.

## PART 6 — SECOND ENCOUNTER
**Suggested duration:** 9–11s
**Story beat:** Zion arrives to pick up Chase and Tory realizes “Green” is her friend's uncle.
**Action/blocking:** Chase complains that Zion never greeted Hazel. He tries to make peace with ice cream, then remembers he is due at the engagement dinner. At the school pickup, Zion and Tory see each other again.
**Dialogue:** “Chase.” / “Uncle, meet my friend, Hazel.”
**Performance:** Both freeze; neither knows what to say.
**End/carry-over:** EP03 starts with the engagement day and Zion's conflict with Gerald.

## CONTINUITY LOCK
- Zion still has no meaningful introduction with Tory.
- Tory calls him “Green” privately because she does not know his name.
- Chase calls Tory Hazel.
- Zion is scheduled to get engaged.
- Gerald is still pressuring him to marry someone chosen by the family.
