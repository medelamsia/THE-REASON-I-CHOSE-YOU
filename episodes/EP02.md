## PART 01 — 00–10
**Story Beat:** GERALD FIGHT
**Action:** Zion leaves after Gerald threatens his position and forces the marriage issue.
**Blocking:** He storms out of the mansion and chooses coffee instead of vodka.
**Dialogue:** "He was forcing me to marry someone I didn't want."

**Subtext:** He wants distance from Gerald and his forced future.

**Props:** Car keys, phone.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Cold, decisive departure.

## PART 02 — 10–20
**Story Beat:** THE WAITRESS
**Action:** At the café Zion notices the short brunette and hears her low voice.
**Blocking:** He watches Tory approach, becomes unsettled by her voice, and walks away.
**Dialogue:** "Sir."
**Character Expression:** Zion: attracted and irritated by the attraction.
**Subtext:** He wants to escape the unexpected pull.
**Environment:** Cheap café/table five.
**Props:** Coffee cup, sunglasses.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** POV framing from Zion; fast exit.

## PART 03 — 20–30
**Story Beat:** SECOND LOOK
**Action:** Tory follows him to the Audi and Zion removes his sunglasses again.
**Blocking:** He turns, looks at Tory, follows her gaze to the Audi, and studies her hair in the wind.
**Dialogue:** "Yeah, sir, your coffee..."
**Character Expression:** Zion: privately captivated, publicly cold.
**Subtext:** He is deciding whether this stranger matters.
**Environment:** Parking area.
**Props:** Audi Q8, sunglasses.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Match the eye reveal from EP01 from a new angle.

## PART 04 — 30–40
**Story Beat:** CHASE
**Action:** Tory visits Amelia at school and reconnects warmly with Chase.
**Blocking:** She hugs Chase, listens to his news, and learns her uncle is back and getting married.
**Dialogue:** "I missed you so much."
**Character Expression:** Tory: relaxed and affectionate.
**Subtext:** She wants time with Chase and Amelia, not romance.
**Environment:** School grounds.
**Props:** School bag, bicycle if present.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Warm wider framing to contrast Zion POV.

## PART 05 — 40–50
**Story Beat:** HAZEL
**Action:** Zion arrives to pick Chase up. Tory recognizes him as the café stranger.
**Blocking:** Zion approaches; Chase calls Tory Hazel and introduces them indirectly.

**Props:** Car keys, school bag.
