# EP02 — ZION POV + CHASE / ENGAGEMENT SETUP

## DIRECTOR BRIEF
**Dramatic Purpose:** Reframe the café encounter through Zion, then carry the connection through Chase into the engagement day without repeating Chapter 3's Hazel introduction twice.
**Runtime:** HARD MAXIMUM 60 seconds.
**Part Timing:** six continuous 10-second production splits.
**Part Rule:** This is one continuous adaptation flow. No story reset at part boundaries and no duplicate beat in the next episode.

## PART 01 — 00:00–00:10
**Story Beat:** ZION LEAVES GERALD
**Action:** Zion storms out after Gerald demands the arranged marriage and threatens his company position.
**Blocking:** Mansion interior → driveway → car.
**Dialogue:** “He was forcing me to marry someone I didn't want.” / “I decided to go for a cup of coffee since it was too early for vodka.”
**Character Expression:** Angry, controlled, restless.
**Subtext:** He wants one decision in his life that is his.
**Environment:** Davit mansion/driveway.
**Props:** Phone, car keys, sunglasses.
**Continuity:** He leaves carrying the marriage conflict into the café.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Hard departure cut; do not overexplain Gerald.

## PART 02 — 00:10–00:20
**Story Beat:** SAME CAFÉ / TORY'S VOICE
**Action:** Zion waits impatiently, sees Tory approach, studies her, hears her low voice and becomes unexpectedly affected.
**Blocking:** Zion seated at table five; Tory approaches from the café floor.
**Dialogue:** “Sir.”
**Character Expression:** Zion goes from irritation to involuntary attention.
**Subtext:** He wants to leave before the attraction becomes a problem.
**Environment:** Cheap café, table five.
**Props:** Coffee, sunglasses.
**Continuity:** He deliberately walks away before taking the coffee.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** POV echo of EP01, but his gaze—not Tory's—is the storytelling focus.

## PART 03 — 00:20–00:30
**Story Beat:** GREEN EYES / ESCAPE
**Action:** Tory follows him to the Audi; Zion turns, removes the sunglasses, and notices her natural brunette hair.
**Blocking:** Parking area; Tory remains a few steps behind him until he turns.
**Dialogue:** “Yeah, sir, your coffee. You didn't even touch it...”
**Character Expression:** Zion intrigued but guarded; Tory fascinated.
**Subtext:** Zion is registering exactly the feature that will matter later, while pretending he is not.
**Environment:** Café parking area.
**Props:** Audi Q8, sunglasses, coffee.
**Continuity:** Zion drives away; Tory is left staring.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Match the green-eye reveal from EP01 without replaying the same coverage.

## PART 04 — 00:30–00:40
**Story Beat:** CHASE CONNECTS THEM
**Action:** Tory visits Amelia at school and spends time with Chase. She learns Chase's uncle is back and is getting married.
**Blocking:** School grounds; Tory moves from Amelia's area to Chase.
**Dialogue:** “I missed you so much.” / “He came. Now he's living here forever.”
**Character Expression:** Tory relaxed and affectionate.
**Subtext:** She is there for family, not because she is looking for Zion.
**Environment:** School.
**Props:** School bag, car.
**Continuity:** Zion will arrive as Chase's uncle.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Warm family coverage to contrast the cold Zion POV.

## PART 05 — 00:40–00:50
**Story Beat:** SECOND ENCOUNTER
**Action:** Zion arrives to pick up Chase. Tory and Zion recognize each other; Chase introduces Tory as Hazel.
**Blocking:** Chase between them; both adults stop for a beat.
**Dialogue:** “Uncle, meet my friend, Hazel.”
**Character Expression:** Mutual shock and awkward attraction.
**Subtext:** Each realizes the stranger from the café is now inside the family orbit.
**Environment:** School pickup area.
**Props:** Car keys, school bag.
**Continuity:** Zion leaves with Chase; Tory remains with Amelia/Chase's social world.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** One clean eyeline exchange; do not extend the awkwardness.

## PART 06 — 00:50–01:00
**Story Beat:** ENGAGEMENT PRESSURE
**Action:** Zion returns to the mansion and faces Gerald about the engagement. He still wants Nataly.
**Blocking:** Mansion entry/study; Gerald blocks his path or confronts him directly.
**Dialogue:** “I won't do it.” / “I want to marry Nataly.”
**Character Expression:** Zion defiant but cornered.
**Subtext:** He believes he can still control which woman becomes his wife.
**Environment:** Davit mansion.
**Props:** Phone, jacket, keys.
**Continuity:** EP03 begins on the engagement day with Amelia preparing to become Zion's bride.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** End on Zion's refusal, not on another Hazel reveal, because that beat has already been established.
