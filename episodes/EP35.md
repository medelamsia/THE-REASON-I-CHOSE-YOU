# EP35 — NATALY / HOSPITAL CRISIS

## DIRECTOR BRIEF
**Dramatic Purpose:** Nataly's revelation about the past pregnancy changes Zion's view of fatherhood; the unresolved Nana emergency then becomes a hospital crisis that finally exposes Tory's pregnancy.
**Runtime:** HARD MAXIMUM 60 seconds.
**Part Timing:** six continuous 10-second production splits. They are production partitions, not separate scenes. No reset or filler.

## PART 01 — 00:00–00:10
**Story Beat:** NATALY'S CANCER
**Action:** Zion drives Nataly to her medical appointment and stays involved because she has cancer.
**Blocking:** Car interior → hospital arrival; Zion is focused on Nataly and his phone.
**Dialogue:** Use the established appointment dialogue.
**Character Expression:** Zion distracted and concerned.
**Subtext:** He is trying to help someone from his past without abandoning his present life.
**Environment:** Car and hospital entrance.
**Props:** Phone, medical appointment items.
**Continuity:** He still has the house key and missed calls waiting from Tory.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Keep this compressed; the important information is Nataly's illness.

## PART 02 — 00:10–00:20
**Story Beat:** THE OLD BABY
**Action:** Nataly reveals that she had an abortion involving Zion's baby.
**Blocking:** Keep them facing each other; Zion stops moving when the confession lands.
**Dialogue:** “I had an abortion.”
**Character Expression:** Zion stunned, then angry and hurt.
**Subtext:** He suddenly confronts a version of fatherhood he once rejected.
**Environment:** Hospital/car conversation.
**Props:** Phone, medical paperwork.
**Continuity:** Zion leaves the conversation thinking about Tory and their baby.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Hold briefly on the confession/reaction; do not turn it into a long flashback.

## PART 03 — 00:20–00:30
**Story Beat:** HOUSE KEY
**Action:** Zion finds the house key he had prepared as his anniversary gift and realizes it is the home where their children could grow up.
**Blocking:** He opens the small box, turns the key in his fingers, then checks his phone.
**Dialogue:** No invented line; let the key and internal realization carry the beat.
**Character Expression:** Surprise becomes genuine excitement about Tory's baby.
**Subtext:** For the first time he emotionally pictures himself as a father.
**Environment:** Zion's office/household space.
**Props:** Small box, house key, phone, 56 missed calls/voicemail.
**Continuity:** The voicemail from Tory about Nana becomes urgent.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Insert on key → missed calls → immediate decision to go.

## PART 04 — 00:30–00:40
**Story Beat:** NANA EMERGENCY
**Action:** Zion hears Tory's voicemail saying Nana has been rushed to the hospital and heads there immediately.
**Blocking:** Phone → keys → car → hospital; do not linger on travel.
**Dialogue:** “Baby... it's Nana. She's... she's been rushed to the hospital. I'm scared. Please come to the hospital.”
**Character Expression:** Panic and guilt.
**Subtext:** He knows he was unavailable when Tory needed him.
**Environment:** Car/hospital route.
**Props:** Phone, keys.
**Continuity:** Tory is waiting at the hospital with family; Zion still has not explained everything.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Use sound/phone as bridge; hard cut into hospital confrontation.

## PART 05 — 00:40–00:50
**Story Beat:** TORY CONFRONTS ZION
**Action:** Zion reaches Tory. She is grieving and furious that he was absent while Gustavo managed to arrive.
**Blocking:** Tory faces Zion; he approaches carefully; Brenda remains nearby.
**Dialogue:** “Where the fuck were you?” / “Gustavo was in another fucking state, but he made it here.”
**Character Expression:** Tory devastated and furious; Zion guilty.
**Subtext:** Her anger is about abandonment at a moment of crisis.
**Environment:** Hospital/family waiting area.
**Props:** Phone, hospital items.
**Continuity:** Tory is pregnant but Zion still has not been told by her.

## PART 06 — 00:50–01:00
**Story Beat:** PREGNANCY REVEAL / BLEEDING
**Action:** Tory cries out with stomach pain, Amelia blurts that she is pregnant, Tory looks down and sees blood, then Zion carries her into treatment.
**Blocking:** Tory clutches stomach → family reacts → Tory collapses → Zion lifts her → doctors take over.
**Dialogue:** “She's pregnant!” / “If anything happens to my baby...” / “Nothing will happen to our baby. Help!”
**Character Expression:** Shock turns to terror; Zion's anger disappears into fear.
**Subtext:** The secret is no longer hers alone; the survival of the baby now becomes everyone's immediate concern.
**Environment:** Hospital ward/emergency treatment area.
**Props:** Clothing, hospital bed, medical equipment.
**Continuity:** EP36 begins with the doctors explaining the subchorionic hemorrhage and the strong heartbeat.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Fast escalation; end on the hospital doors closing.
