# EPISODE 04 — CHOICE

## DIRECTOR BRIEF
**Runtime:** hard max 60s. Target ~50–58s.
**Story function:** payoff the first three episodes. Zion recognizes Tory as “the little waitress” and changes the marriage choice.
## PART 1 — THE DAVITS ARRIVE
**Suggested duration:** 8s
**Story beat:** The Davit family arrives but Zion is late.
**Action/blocking:** Tory is sent downstairs to serve. She puts on a cheerful face for guests despite hating the situation.
**Dialogue:** “Of course, Dad. Is the groom here yet?” / “No.”
**Performance:** Tory's public smile is deliberately fake.

## PART 2 — CHASE'S HAZEL
**Suggested duration:** 8s
**Story beat:** Chase runs to Tory and introduces her to Brenda.
**Action/blocking:** Chase hugs Tory around the waist; Brenda warmly embraces her. Chase asks whether Tory is her aunty.
**Dialogue:** “Hazel!” / “You must be Chase's Hazel.” / “Are you my aunty?”
**Performance:** Tory is uncomfortable but touched that Chase talks about her.

## PART 3 — DINNER / RECOGNITION
**Suggested duration:** 9s
**Story beat:** Tory eats while everyone else barely touches the meal, then realizes Green is her future brother-in-law.
**Action/blocking:** Tory notices the untouched plates, serves herself more food, checks on Amelia, then is told Zion has arrived.
**Dialogue:** “He's here.”
**Performance:** Humor briefly relieves the tension.

## PART 4 — THE STARE
**Suggested duration:** 9s
**Story beat:** Zion enters and cannot take his eyes off Tory.
**Action/blocking:** Tory and Amelia walk downstairs holding hands. Zion's eyes stay on Tory. Amelia notices.
**Dialogue:** No new dialogue needed; use the source's silence as pressure.
**Performance:** Tory's protective glare says “don't hurt my sister.”

## PART 5 — PRIVATE DECISION
**Suggested duration:** 10–12s
**Story beat:** Zion changes his mind and tells Gerald he wants the younger sister.
**Action/blocking:** Zion pulls Gerald aside. He says he wants Tory. Gerald protests about her age, then agrees because she is from the same family.
**Dialogue:** “I want the younger one.” / “She's twenty-two.” / “It's her or I don't marry.”
**Performance:** Zion is decisive; Gerald is furious.

## PART 6 — PUBLIC CHOICE
**Suggested duration:** 8–10s
**Story beat:** Zion tells both families he will marry Tory; Amelia breaks down.
**Action/blocking:** Zion approaches the sisters. He says “I will marry,” pauses long enough for Amelia to relax, then points at Tory. Amelia cries and leaves. Tory starts after her, stops, returns, and says “Fuck you.”
**Dialogue:** “I will marry.” / “But I will marry you.” / “Fuck you.”
**End/carry-over:** EP05 opens on the wedding day.
**Performance:** Do not play Tory's insult as cute flirting. It is anger.

## CONTINUITY LOCK
- Tory is now the selected bride.
- Amelia has been publicly rejected.
- Zion chose Tory after recognizing her from the café/school.
- Tory's loyalty to Amelia remains stronger than her attraction to Zion.
