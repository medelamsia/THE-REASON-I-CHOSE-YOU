# EP04 — THE BRIDE SWITCH

## DIRECTOR BRIEF
**Dramatic Purpose:** Make Zion's choice of Tory the irreversible inciting decision.
**Runtime:** HARD MAXIMUM 60 seconds.
**Part Timing:** 00:00–00:10, 00:10–00:20, 00:20–00:30, 00:30–00:40, 00:40–00:50, 00:50–01:00.
**Part Rule:** These are continuous 10-second production splits, not standalone scenes. Story, blocking and emotion continue through cuts. Never slow the story to fill a part.

## PART 01 — 00–10
**Story Beat:** DAVITS
**Action:** Tory serves the Davits and interacts with Brenda and Chase.
**Blocking:** Chase hugs Tory; Brenda recognizes her as Hazel; the family settles into dinner.
**Dialogue:** "You must be Chase's Hazel."
**Character Expression:** Tory: awkward but warm; Brenda: welcoming.
**Subtext:** Tory wants to survive the evening for Amelia.
**Environment:** Wallsmans' dining room.
**Props:** Serving tray, plates.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Family-group framing, then isolate Tory.

## PART 02 — 10–20
**Story Beat:** REALIZATION
**Action:** Tory connects Green to the man Amelia is supposed to marry.
**Blocking:** She looks between Chase, Brenda and the empty groom's seat, then realizes the connection.
**Dialogue:** "Are you my aunty?"
**Character Expression:** Tory: dawning shock.
**Subtext:** She does not want the stranger to hurt Amelia.
**Environment:** Dining room.
**Props:** Wedding/engagement place settings.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Use eyeline chain to reveal realization.

## PART 03 — 20–30
**Story Beat:** ZION ARRIVES
**Action:** Zion enters and locks eyes with Tory as the sisters descend together.
**Blocking:** He watches Tory first, then the sisters arrive holding hands.
**Dialogue:** No added line necessary.
**Character Expression:** Zion: immediate recognition; Tory: guarded.
**Subtext:** Zion has found the person from the café again.
**Environment:** Foyer/staircase.
**Props:** Coat, phone.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Slow only for the recognition; then move quickly.

## PART 04 — 30–40
**Story Beat:** PRIVATE CHOICE
**Action:** Zion pulls Gerald aside and says he wants the younger sister.
**Blocking:** He states the change privately while Gerald challenges her age and the plan.
**Dialogue:** "I want the younger one." / "She's twenty-two."
**Character Expression:** Zion: decisive; Gerald: furious.
**Subtext:** Zion wants Tory; Gerald wants the arrangement intact.
**Environment:** Mansion side room/hall.
**Props:** Phone, suit jacket.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Over-the-shoulder confrontation.

## PART 05 — 40–50
**Story Beat:** PUBLIC CHOICE
**Action:** Zion returns to the group and announces he will marry Tory.
**Blocking:** He pauses, points to Tory, and says the choice aloud while Amelia collapses emotionally.
**Dialogue:** "But I will marry you."
**Character Expression:** Tory: stunned; Amelia: devastated; Zion: resolute.
**Subtext:** The original deal is rewritten in one sentence.
**Environment:** Dining room.
**Props:** Wedding ring/hand gestures.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Cut between faces at the announcement; no slow speech.

## PART 06 — 50–60
**Story Beat:** AFTERMATH
**Action:** Amelia leaves crying. Tory follows, then returns only long enough to confront Zion.
**Blocking:** Tory stands face-to-face with him and delivers the insult before leaving again.
**Dialogue:** "Fuck you."
**Character Expression:** Tory: furious, not flirtatious.
**Subtext:** She wants to protect Amelia and reject being selected.
**Environment:** Hall/foyer.
**Props:** None required.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Hard cut on the insult into wedding prep.
