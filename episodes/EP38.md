# EP38 — SIX MONTHS / MISUNDERSTANDING

## DIRECTOR BRIEF
**Dramatic Purpose:** At six months pregnant Tory wants normal independence; Zion's protective work restrictions collide with Nataly's messages and create the final misunderstanding.
**Runtime:** HARD MAXIMUM 60 seconds.
**Part Timing:** six continuous 10-second production partitions. They are continuous production chunks, not independent scenes.

## PART 01 — 00:00–00:10
**Story Beat:** WORK ARGUMENT
**Action:** Tory insists on going to work. Zion tells her to rest; she throws her phone, chases him, and forces him to agree to half-days.
**Blocking:** Bedroom/home; playful chase with Zion stopping her from running too hard.
**Dialogue:** “But I wanna go to work!” / “My love, you have to stay home and rest.” / “I'm pregnant, not disabled!”
**Character Expression:** Tory stubborn and energetic; Zion exasperated but protective.
**Subtext:** Tory wants autonomy; Zion fears overexertion.
**Environment:** Home/bedroom.
**Props:** Phone, work items.
**Continuity:** They leave for the company together.

## PART 02 — 00:10–00:20
**Story Beat:** PUBLIC PREGNANCY
**Action:** Tory and Zion enter the company holding hands; the receptionist congratulates them, establishing that the pregnancy is now public at Éclaire.
**Blocking:** Reception → elevator/office; Tory visibly proud.
**Dialogue:** “Good morning, Mr. Davit. Welcome back, Mrs. Davit, and congratulations.”
**Character Expression:** Tory proud; Zion composed with hidden amusement.
**Subtext:** The baby is no longer a secret at work.
**Environment:** Éclaire reception/office.
**Props:** Phone, work bag, laptop.
**Continuity:** Zion prepares a rest station for Tory in his office.

## PART 03 — 00:20–00:30
**Story Beat:** PROTECTIVE WORK SETUP
**Action:** Zion arranges pillows and a foot stand so Tory can work from the sofa and removes unnecessary work from her schedule.
**Blocking:** Office sofa; Tory sits and opens her laptop while Zion adjusts the space.
**Dialogue:** “Not a chance.” / “It's this or home.” / “Because you removed everything from it.”
**Character Expression:** Tory annoyed; Zion amused and firm.
**Subtext:** He is caring but controlling; she wants to prove she can still function professionally.
**Environment:** Zion's office.
**Props:** Pillows, stand, laptop, phone.
**Continuity:** They finish the half-day and go shopping.

## PART 04 — 00:30–00:40
**Story Beat:** NATALY MESSAGE
**Action:** Nataly messages Zion about an appointment. He hides the phone from Tory and then takes her shopping instead.
**Blocking:** Office → exit → car → store.
**Dialogue:** “Everything okay?” / “Yep.” / “And some baby clothes?”
**Character Expression:** Zion distracted; Tory observant.
**Subtext:** Zion is trying to manage Nataly's medical problem privately while prioritizing Tory.
**Environment:** Car/store.
**Props:** Phone, shopping bags.
**Continuity:** Tory still trusts him but is now sensitive to secrecy.

## PART 05 — 00:40–00:50
**Story Beat:** “OUR BABY”
**Action:** While shopping, Tory notices Zion replying to Nataly. Later at night, Nataly's call comes in; Tory answers and sees the messages about “our baby.”
**Blocking:** Store → home → bedroom/bathroom at night; phone becomes the key prop.
**Dialogue:** Nataly: “Is it about our baby?” / Tory's internal reaction: “They had a baby together?”
**Character Expression:** Curiosity → shock → betrayal.
**Subtext:** Tory fills missing information with the worst possible explanation.
**Environment:** Home at night.
**Props:** Zion's phone, wedding ring.
**Continuity:** Tory now believes Zion has another child with Nataly.

## PART 06 — 00:50–01:00
**Story Beat:** RING / DEPARTURE
**Action:** Tory removes her wedding ring, leaves Zion's phone beside it, packs, waits until early morning to avoid security interference, then drives away.
**Blocking:** Bedroom floor → packing → security area → car.
**Dialogue:** “But he doesn't deserve us.” / “Yeah, you were there at the mall too.”
**Character Expression:** Tory heartbroken but determined; Zion wakes confused and then panics.
**Subtext:** Tory is protecting herself and her baby from what she believes is betrayal.
**Environment:** Mansion/driveway, early morning.
**Props:** Wedding ring, phone, bags, car.
**Continuity:** Zion holds the ring and phone and orders the car tracked; EP39 starts with Tory at the hotel.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Ring + phone close-up, then hard cut to car leaving.
