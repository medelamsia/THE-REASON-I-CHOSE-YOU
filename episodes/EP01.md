# EPISODE 01 — CHAPTER 1

## DIRECTOR BRIEF
**Runtime:** hard max 60s. Target ~50–55s.
**Story function:** perkenalan Tory, café world, first visual collision with Zion, first involuntary attraction.
**Episode must flow directly into EP02.**
**Title:** optional packaging only.

## PART 1 — MORNING RUSH
**Suggested duration:** 5–7s
**Story beat:** Eight years later, Tory is 22 and already late for work.
**Action/blocking:** Start inside the home on Tory finishing her morning routine; she realizes the time, stuffs food into her backpack, grabs her bike, and rushes out. Keep movement continuous into the street.
**Dialogue:** No new dialogue. Narration is optional only if the format later requires it; the visual itself should establish “late.”
**Performance:** Energetic, slightly chaotic, self-aware.
**Director:** Handheld-feeling morning movement; no establishing montage that wastes time.

## PART 2 — CAFÉ BANTER
**Suggested duration:** 8–10s
**Story beat:** Nana catches Tory being late; Tory immediately charms her way out.
**Action/blocking:** Tory enters, freezes at Nana's stare, approaches the counter, then pivots into exaggerated praise about Nana's new hair. Zach protests that he complimented her too.
**Dialogue:** “Late? Again?” / “I'm sorry, Nana... traffic.” / “Bikes can get caught up in traffic nowadays?” / “Nana, did you just do your hair? You look so fabulous.”
**Performance:** Tory lies badly but confidently; Nana is dry and unimpressed; Zach is playful.
**Director:** Keep this quick. The humor comes from reaction cuts, not long pauses.

## PART 3 — TABLE FIVE
**Suggested duration:** 7–9s
**Story beat:** Tory gets the order for table five and notices the strange customer.
**Action/blocking:** Zach hands Tory the coffee. Tory slips into apron, takes the cup, and crosses the café toward table five. She stops when she sees a very tall man in a full suit and sunglasses.
**Dialogue:** “Hey, sunflower... table number five?” / “Sure.” / “Don't push it, Zee.”
**Performance:** Zach is delighted by the nickname; Tory laughs while walking away.
**Director:** Reveal Zion from Tory's perspective. Hold his full silhouette only long enough for the contrast to register.

## PART 4 — THE CHASE
**Suggested duration:** 9–11s
**Story beat:** Zion walks away before Tory can serve him, forcing her into an awkward chase.
**Action/blocking:** Tory reaches the table, says “Sir,” and Zion immediately passes her. Tory loses balance, looks toward Nana, understands the signal, grabs the coffee, and runs after him. Her slippers slap against the pavement.
**Dialogue:** “Sir...” / “Sir!”
**Performance:** Tory moves from stunned → offended → determined.
**Director:** Use quick tracking shots. The comedy is the speed difference between them. Do not turn the chase into a long running sequence.

## PART 5 — GREEN EYES
**Suggested duration:** 10–12s
**Story beat:** Tory catches Zion at his Audi Q8 and sees his eyes for the first time.
**Action/blocking:** Tory collides with his back. Zion turns. Tory's eyes drop briefly toward the Audi, then Zion follows her gaze, looks back, and removes his sunglasses. His green eyes become the visual reveal.
**Dialogue:** “Eenh... enhrm.”
**Performance:** Zion stays composed; Tory's brain visibly shuts down.
**Director:** This is the episode's visual payoff. Push in briefly on the green eyes, then cut immediately to Tory's stunned face. Do not over-romanticize; the humor must survive.

## PART 6 — BACK TO REALITY
**Suggested duration:** 9–11s
**Story beat:** Tory remembers the coffee, rambles, gets ignored, and returns to the café humiliated.
**Action/blocking:** Tory thrusts the coffee forward and rambles about the café's coffee. Zion checks his Rolex, gets into the car, and leaves. Tory returns inside and finds the staff staring.
**Dialogue:** “Your coffee, sir. You didn't even touch it...” / “I am green... I mean, fine.” / Zach: “Are you Elphaba now?”
**Performance:** Tory tries to recover dignity; the staff's laughter destroys it.
**End/carry-over:** EP02 picks up with Zion's POV and explains what happened inside his head during this exact encounter.

## CONTINUITY LOCK
- Tory is 22.
- Tory works at the café and is known for making coffee.
- Zach is her coworker; Nana is her boss.
- Tory and Zion have not exchanged names.
- Zion is introduced visually as very tall, suited, sunglasses, green eyes.
- Tory is attracted despite finding him rude.
- No added romantic confession, backstory exposition, or new event.

## PACING LOCK
The six parts are editorial units, not six 10-second scenes. Keep the episode around 50–55s unless performance naturally needs less. Never pad to reach 60s.
