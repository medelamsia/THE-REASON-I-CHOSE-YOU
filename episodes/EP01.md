# EPISODE 01 — GREEN EYES

Source anchor: Chapter 1
Runtime target: ~60 seconds
Format: 6 × 10-second blocks
POV: Tory
Hook: The rude stranger finally removes his sunglasses — and Tory forgets how to speak.

## Logline
Late for work, Tory is ordered to deliver coffee to an unusually intimidating customer. He leaves without touching the drink, forcing her to chase him outside. The moment he reveals his green eyes, her entire attitude short-circuits.

## BLOCK 1 — LATE AGAIN
0:00–0:10

Visual: Morning. Tory arrives at the small café on a bicycle, slightly breathless, backpack over one shoulder. Nana watches from behind the counter.

Action: Tory slips inside and freezes when Nana raises an eyebrow.

Dialogue:
NANA: "Late again?"
TORY: "Traffic."
NANA: "On a bicycle?"

Tone: Fast, grounded comedy. Natural imperfections.

## BLOCK 2 — TABLE FIVE
0:10–0:20

Visual: Café kitchen. Tory ties on her apron. Zach hands her a coffee tray.

Action: He grins and points toward table five.

Dialogue:
ZACH: "Sunflower, table five. Help your best friend."
TORY: "Don't push it, Zee."

Beat: Zach celebrates the nickname like it is a major life event. Tory laughs under her breath.

## BLOCK 3 — THE STRANGER
0:20–0:30

Visual: Tory reaches table five and stops.

Action: Zion sits alone in a dark suit and sunglasses despite the warm weather. He is noticeably taller than everyone around him.

Dialogue:
TORY (under her breath): "Who wears a whole suit in this heat?"

Action: She approaches. Before she can finish, Zion stands and walks straight past her.

Tory reaction: Stunned, then offended.

## BLOCK 4 — DON'T LET HIM LEAVE
0:30–0:40

Visual: Zion strides toward the parking area. Tory looks back at Nana. Nana gives her the unmistakable "go after him" look.

Action: Tory sighs, grabs the untouched coffee, and runs after him.

Dialogue:
TORY: "Sir!"
No response.
TORY: "Sir!"

Sound: Slapping slippers/quick footsteps, restrained comedy.

## BLOCK 5 — GREEN EYES
0:40–0:50

Visual: Tory accidentally bumps into Zion's back beside his car.

Action: Zion turns. He removes his sunglasses.

Camera: Push in on his green eyes, then cut to Tory's stunned face.

Dialogue:
ZION: "..."
TORY: "Enh... enhrm."

Beat: Tory realizes she has completely lost her train of thought.

## BLOCK 6 — SHE'S GONE
0:50–1:00

Visual: Tory thrusts the coffee forward, rambling while Zion checks his watch.

Dialogue:
TORY: "Your coffee. You didn't touch it. We make the best coffee here, so... you might regret—"

Action: Zion gets into his car and drives away.

Cut back to café: Everyone is staring.

ZACH: "You okay?"
TORY: "I'm... green. I mean, fine."

End hook: Zach and the staff burst out laughing. Tory gives him a deadly stare.

On-screen end card: "She didn't know his name yet."

## Continuity notes
- Tory and Zion do not know each other's identities in this episode.
- Zion's green eyes are the first visual detail that genuinely disarms Tory.
- Tory's attraction is involuntary and immediately masked with sarcasm.
- Do not introduce the marriage plot until later episodes.
