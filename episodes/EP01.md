# EP01 — FLASHBACK + FIRST COLLISION

## DIRECTOR BRIEF
**Dramatic Purpose:** Establish the debt that binds the families, then introduce adult Tory and the stranger who will become Zion.
**Runtime:** HARD MAXIMUM 60 seconds.
**Part Timing:** 00:00–00:10, 00:10–00:20, 00:20–00:30, 00:30–00:40, 00:40–00:50, 00:50–01:00.
**Part Rule:** These are continuous 10-second production splits, not standalone scenes. Story, blocking and emotion continue through cuts. Never slow the story to fill a part.

## PART 01 — 00–10
**Story Beat:** DEBT
**Action:** Young Tory overhears that the Davits want one daughter to marry their son because the Wallsmans cannot clear the debt.
**Blocking:** She hides nearby and listens instead of joining the adults.
**Dialogue:** She stays physically out of sight while Amelia faces their father.
**Character Expression:** "They want one of you."
**Subtext:** Tory: frightened, incredulous.
**Environment:** This is bigger than chores; her family's future is being negotiated.
**Props:** Wallsmans' home, dinner time, family room.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Dinnerware, doorway/hidden corner.

## PART 02 — 10–20
**Story Beat:** LATE
**Action:** Eight years later Tory is 22, oversleeps/starts late and races to the café by bicycle.
**Blocking:** She packs food, grabs the bike, rides fast, enters the café still catching breath.
**Dialogue:** "Late? Again?" / "There was... traffic."
**Character Expression:** Tory: shamelessly improvising an excuse.
**Subtext:** She needs to get through the shift without admitting she is irresponsible.
**Environment:** Café entrance and counter.
**Props:** Backpack, bicycle, apron.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Fast cuts between clock/door/bike to preserve comic pace.

## PART 03 — 20–30
**Story Beat:** CAFÉ
**Action:** Nana teases Tory about the bike excuse; Tory charms her with compliments and Zach joins the banter.
**Blocking:** Tory approaches Nana, pivots into praise, then moves to the kitchen.
**Dialogue:** "You look so fabulous." / "Don't push it, Zee."
**Character Expression:** Tory playful; Nana dry; Zach delighted.
**Subtext:** Tory wants to escape the scolding and get back to work.
**Environment:** Busy café counter/kitchen.
**Props:** Apron, coffee orders.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Quick shot-reverse-shot; keep work rhythm visible.

## PART 04 — 30–40
**Story Beat:** TABLE 5
**Action:** Zach sends Tory to table five. She sees a very tall suited man in sunglasses and freezes.
**Blocking:** Tory carries coffee across the room, stops at the table, studies him, then tries to recover.
**Dialogue:** "Sir..."
**Character Expression:** Tory shifts from curiosity to intimidation.
**Subtext:** She needs to serve him before he leaves, but he is physically overwhelming.
**Environment:** Café dining area, table five.
**Props:** Coffee cup, tray.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Reveal Zion from Tory's eyeline; emphasize scale without lingering.

## PART 05 — 40–50
**Story Beat:** CHASE
**Action:** The stranger walks out. Nana's signal reminds Tory of the café's 'don't let an angry customer leave' rule.
**Blocking:** Tory runs after him, slippers striking the pavement, and collides with his back at the Audi.
**Dialogue:** "Sir!"
**Character Expression:** Tory determined; Zion unreadable.
**Subtext:** She must complete the customer interaction even if he is rude.
**Environment:** Café exterior/parking area.
**Props:** Coffee cup, car keys, Audi Q8.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Tracking movement; cut on collision.

## PART 06 — 50–60
**Story Beat:** GREEN EYES
**Action:** Zion turns, removes sunglasses, and Tory sees his green eyes for the first time.
**Blocking:** Tory steps back, looks from the Audi to Zion, then locks onto his eyes. Zion checks his Rolex and drives away.
**Dialogue:** "I am green... I mean, fine."
**Character Expression:** Tory visibly short-circuits; Zion remains composed.
**Subtext:** Tory wants to recover dignity after being stunned.
**Environment:** Parking area beside Audi.
**Props:** Sunglasses, Rolex, coffee.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Brief eye close-up, then snap back to Tory's comic humiliation.
