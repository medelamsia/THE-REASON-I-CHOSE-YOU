# EP01 — FLASHBACK + FIRST COLLISION

## DIRECTOR BRIEF
**Dramatic Purpose:** Establish the Wallsmans' debt-driven marriage problem, then jump eight years to Tory's first encounter with Zion, so the audience understands both the stakes and the attraction seed.
**Runtime:** HARD MAXIMUM 60 seconds.
**Part Timing:** 00:00–00:10, 00:10–00:20, 00:20–00:30, 00:30–00:40, 00:40–00:50, 00:50–01:00.
**Part Rule:** Six continuous production splits for downstream storyboard work. They are not standalone scenes. The time split must never force filler; multiple shots can occur within a part.

## PART 01 — 00:00–00:10
**Story Beat:** DEBT REVEAL
**Action:** Fourteen-year-old Tory hides near the family conversation and hears her father say the Wallsmans cannot repay the Davits.
**Blocking:** Tory stays around the corner/hidden while Amelia faces their father.
**Dialogue:** “They want one of you!” / “They want their son to marry one of you.”
**Character Expression:** Tory moves from casual curiosity to shock.
**Subtext:** She realizes an adult decision may determine where the family lives and who her sister becomes.
**Environment:** Family home during dinner; quiet private corner.
**Props:** Dinnerware, household furniture.
**Continuity:** End with Tory retreating to bed emotionally shaken; this is the wound carried into the time jump.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Start tight on Tory listening, then reveal the emotional impact; hard time-jump cut rather than lingering flashback.

## PART 02 — 00:10–00:20
**Story Beat:** EIGHT YEARS LATER / LATE
**Action:** Tory, now 22, realizes she is late, packs food and rides her bicycle to the café.
**Blocking:** Bed/bathroom → kitchen/bag → bike → café entrance.
**Dialogue:** “Late? Again?” / “There was... traffic.”
**Character Expression:** Embarrassed, then instantly improvisational.
**Subtext:** She wants Nana annoyed for only a moment, not a real confrontation.
**Environment:** Tory's home and café entrance.
**Props:** Backpack, food, bicycle, apron.
**Continuity:** She enters the café still carrying the chaotic morning energy.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Fast inserts of time/bag/bike/door to compress eight years plus the morning rush.

## PART 03 — 00:20–00:30
**Story Beat:** CAFÉ RHYTHM
**Action:** Nana calls Tory late; Tory flatters Nana's hair; Zach jokes with her; Tory starts work.
**Blocking:** Counter → Nana → Zach → kitchen; keep staff moving behind them.
**Dialogue:** “Bikes can get caught up in traffic nowadays?” / “You look so fabulous.” / “Don't push it, Zee.”
**Character Expression:** Tory playful and shameless; Nana dry; Zach exuberant.
**Subtext:** Tory escapes the scolding by turning the room into a joke.
**Environment:** Busy café.
**Props:** Apron, coffee orders, counter items.
**Continuity:** Zach then hands Tory the coffee for table five.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Quick reaction cuts and moving background activity; no static setup shot.

## PART 04 — 00:30–00:40
**Story Beat:** TABLE FIVE
**Action:** Tory carries coffee to table five and sees the unusually tall suited man in sunglasses.
**Blocking:** Kitchen → dining floor → stop at table five; he remains seated while his size dominates the frame.
**Dialogue:** “Sir...”
**Character Expression:** Tory intimidated and curious.
**Subtext:** She wants to finish serving him before he leaves but cannot stop staring.
**Environment:** Café dining area in summer.
**Props:** Coffee cup/tray, sunglasses.
**Continuity:** Zion walks past her before she can serve him, creating the chase.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Reveal him through Tory's eyeline; use scale contrast, not a long hero shot.

## PART 05 — 00:40–00:50
**Story Beat:** THE CHASE
**Action:** Nana gives Tory the “you know what to do” look; Tory runs outside after him and collides with his back beside the Audi.
**Blocking:** Café → doorway → pavement/parking area → collision.
**Dialogue:** “Sir!”
**Character Expression:** Tory determined; Zion controlled.
**Subtext:** Tory is following café policy, not intentionally flirting.
**Environment:** Café exterior/parking area.
**Props:** Coffee cup, Audi Q8.
**Continuity:** Zion turns toward her; his sunglasses are still on.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Tracking run; cut on collision.

## PART 06 — 00:50–01:00
**Story Beat:** GREEN EYES
**Action:** Zion removes his sunglasses, Tory sees his green eyes, rambles about the untouched coffee; he checks his Rolex and drives away.
**Blocking:** Tory directly in front of him; Zion controls the interaction; then enters Audi.
**Dialogue:** “Your coffee, sir. You didn't even touch it...” / “I am green... I mean, fine.” / “Are you okay?” / “Are you Elphaba now?”
**Character Expression:** Tory visibly short-circuits; Zion remains unreadable.
**Subtext:** Tory wants to recover dignity after being stunned; Zion is intrigued but gives her nothing.
**Environment:** Parking area beside Audi.
**Props:** Sunglasses, Rolex, coffee, car.
**Continuity:** Tory returns inside fixated on his green eyes; EP02 starts from Zion's side of the same encounter.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Brief eye close-up, immediate reaction, then snap back to comedy; use the eye reveal as the episode's visual hook.
