# EP05 — WEDDING

## DIRECTOR BRIEF
**Dramatic Purpose:** Legalize the relationship and establish the first public signs of possessiveness.
**Runtime:** HARD MAXIMUM 60 seconds.
**Part Timing:** 00:00–00:10, 00:10–00:20, 00:20–00:30, 00:30–00:40, 00:40–00:50, 00:50–01:00.
**Part Rule:** These are continuous 10-second production splits, not standalone scenes. Story, blocking and emotion continue through cuts. Never slow the story to fill a part.

## PART 01 — 00–10
**Story Beat:** DRESS
**Action:** Tory struggles with the wedding dress and heavy makeup.
**Blocking:** She pulls at the dress, cries, and lets Brenda comfort her.
**Dialogue:** "I don't want the makeup."
**Character Expression:** Tory: overwhelmed.
**Subtext:** She wants to remain herself in a marriage she did not choose.
**Environment:** Dressing room.
**Props:** Wedding dress, makeup.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Tight mirror coverage; no glamor montage.

## PART 02 — 10–20
**Story Beat:** RESET
**Action:** Brenda encourages Tory to remove the makeup and choose comfort.
**Blocking:** Tory wipes her face, changes the visual image, and steadies herself.
**Dialogue:** "This is your day."
**Character Expression:** Tory: relief and control return.
**Subtext:** She needs one thing she can choose herself.
**Environment:** Dressing room/mirror.
**Props:** Makeup wipes, mirror.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Hold on her natural face for continuity.

## PART 03 — 20–30
**Story Beat:** AISLE
**Action:** Tory walks with her father while Chase leads the ceremony.
**Blocking:** She approaches Zion, both controlled and tense, and exchanges vows.
**Dialogue:** "I do."
**Character Expression:** Tory/Zion: restrained, not romantic fantasy.
**Subtext:** Both are accepting the legal consequence, not confessing love.
**Environment:** Wedding venue/altar.
**Props:** Bouquet, rings.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Classic forward movement; avoid slow-motion excess.

## PART 04 — 30–40
**Story Beat:** KISS
**Action:** The officiant asks them to kiss. Zion only kisses the corner of Tory's lips.
**Blocking:** He lifts her chin, gives the brief kiss, and whispers his warning.
**Dialogue:** "Don't even think about it."
**Character Expression:** Tory: startled; Zion: controlled teasing.
**Subtext:** The boundary establishes their private dynamic.
**Environment:** Altar.
**Props:** Wedding rings.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Insert on corner kiss, then reaction.

## PART 05 — 40–50
**Story Beat:** GUSTAVO
**Action:** Gustavo flirts at the reception and Tory shows her ring.
**Blocking:** Zion crosses in, places himself between them, and tells Gustavo to leave.
**Dialogue:** "Can't you see I just got married? I'm taken." / "She's my wife, not my dog."
**Character Expression:** Tory: surprised; Zion: jealous.
**Subtext:** Zion's possessiveness appears before he admits attraction.
**Environment:** Reception hall.
**Props:** Ring, champagne glasses if present.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** Quick triangle blocking; no extra romance.

## PART 06 — 50–60
**Story Beat:** JET
**Action:** Zion claims an emergency and sends Tory ahead to the honeymoon.
**Blocking:** Tory boards the jet alone; Linda receives her at destination and reveals clothes were burned.
**Dialogue:** "Where is my stuff?" / "Some of them were... burned."
**Character Expression:** Tory: disbelief turning to outrage.
**Subtext:** She has entered Zion's world but still has no control in it.
**Environment:** Private jet / honeymoon residence.
**Props:** Suitcases, clothes.
**Camera/Visual Intention:** End on Tory's comic outrage.
