# EPISODE 05 — WEDDING

## DIRECTOR BRIEF
**Runtime:** hard max 60s. Target ~52–58s.
**Story function:** convert the family arrangement into a legal marriage and immediately establish the post-wedding power dynamic.
## PART 1 — DRESSED LIKE A DOLL
**Suggested duration:** 8s
**Story beat:** Tory hates the wedding dress and cries after the engagement humiliation.
**Action/blocking:** In the dressing room Tory loosens the gown, grabs snacks, and breaks down. Brenda enters and hugs her.
**Dialogue:** “No. That can't happen.” / “I don't want the makeup.”
**Performance:** Vulnerable, frustrated, not melodramatic.

## PART 2 — TAKE BACK CONTROL
**Suggested duration:** 7s
**Story beat:** Brenda tells Tory she can remove the makeup and be comfortable.
**Action/blocking:** Tory wipes the makeup away and looks at herself in the mirror.
**Dialogue:** “Then get rid of it, girl. This is your day.”
**Performance:** The visual shift from heavy makeup to Tory's natural face is the character beat.

## PART 3 — THE AISLE
**Suggested duration:** 9s
**Story beat:** Tory walks toward Zion and accepts the marriage.
**Action/blocking:** Chase leads as flower girl. Tory walks with her father. Zion waits at the altar.
**Dialogue:** Priest ceremony; “I do.” from Zion and Tory.
**Performance:** Both appear tense and emotionally closed.

## PART 4 — CORNER KISS
**Suggested duration:** 7–8s
**Story beat:** Their first kiss is deliberately restrained.
**Action/blocking:** Zion lifts Tory's chin, she expects a kiss, and he only pecks the corner of her lips.
**Dialogue:** “Don't even think about it.”
**Performance:** Tory blushes; the attraction is involuntary but not yet romantic certainty.
**Director:** Keep the kiss brief; the emotional effect comes from Tory's surprise.

## PART 5 — GUSTAVO
**Suggested duration:** 10s
**Story beat:** Gustavo flirts with Tory at her own reception and Zion immediately reacts.
**Action/blocking:** Gustavo approaches, Tory shows her ring, Zion cuts in, takes Tory's waist, and verbally pushes Gustavo away.
**Dialogue:** “Watch your tongue! Can't you see I just got married?” / “She's my wife, not my dog. Now beat it, Gustavo!”
**Performance:** Zion is jealous and angry; Tory is surprised by the possessiveness.

## PART 6 — JET / LINDA
**Suggested duration:** 10–12s
**Story beat:** Zion leaves Tory to handle an emergency, so she boards the jet alone and meets Linda.
**Action/blocking:** Tory boards, falls asleep, wakes at destination, and meets Linda. Linda explains she is there until Tory has children. Then she reveals some clothes were burned.
**Dialogue:** “Where is my stuff?” / “Some of them were taken to your room and some were burned.” / “He whaaaaaaaaaaaaat?!”
**End/carry-over:** EP06 opens with Zion's POV after the wedding and his meeting with Nataly.
**Performance:** Return to comedy after the emotional wedding beat.

## CONTINUITY LOCK
- Marriage is official.
- Zion and Tory are still emotionally hostile.
- Gustavo is introduced as a jealousy trigger.
- Linda is now attached to Tory's household.
- Tory's dislike of rich-people lifestyle is established.
