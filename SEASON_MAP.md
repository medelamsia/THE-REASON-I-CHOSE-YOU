## Adaptation format
Every episode targets ~60 seconds and uses 6 blocks × 10 seconds. This is a production format, not a claim that one source chapter literally equals one minute.

## Source continuity
The uploaded source's metadata says "Chapters: 52", while its visible table of contents lists numbered chapters through 49, followed by Appreciations and Epilogue. This adaptation follows the numbered chapter sequence shown in the table of contents and uses the Epilogue as the finale.
