# CANON

## Source authority
The uploaded PDF **THE REASON I CHOSE YOU** is the canon source for the adaptation.

The visible table of contents lists Chapter 1–49, Appreciations, and Epilogue. Source metadata separately says "Chapters: 52"; for adaptation purposes, follow the visible numbered chapters and use the Epilogue as the finale.

## Core characters

### Tory Wallsman
- 22 in the main timeline.
- Brunette.
- Prefers trousers and baggy shirts and dislikes dresses.
- Works at a café before entering Éclaire.
- Wants a career as a cosmetic chemist.
- Sassy, blunt, funny, stubborn, emotional, and protective of Amelia.
- Her pregnancy and career become major parts of the later story.

### Amelia Wallsman
- Tory's older sister.
- Blonde.
- Teacher in the main timeline.
- Tory's closest family relationship.
- Becomes pregnant with Gustavo's child and later has Angelo.

### Zion Davit
- 29 in the main timeline.
- Tall, green-eyed, wealthy CEO.
- Initially dating Nataly.
- Is pressured by Gerald into an arranged marriage.
- Chooses Tory instead of Amelia when he recognizes her from the café.
- Has childhood trauma from Gerald and a violent boarding school.
- Has a history of drug use and states that he has been sober for four years.
- Initially rejects fatherhood, later accepts the pregnancy and becomes devoted to Tory and their family.

### Nataly
- Zion's former girlfriend.
- Works with/around Zion at Éclaire after their breakup.
- Has cancer later in the story.
- Reveals that she had an abortion involving Zion's baby before the events of the final pregnancy arc.

### Gustavo
- Zion's longtime rival.
- Becomes involved with Amelia and fathers Angelo.
- His presence repeatedly triggers Zion's jealousy.

### Gerald Davit
- Zion's father.
- Uses business control and the debt arrangement to pressure Zion.
- Abuses Katherine.
- His actions are a major source of Zion's trauma.

### Katherine
- Zion's mother.
- Has endured Gerald's abuse.
- Later gathers evidence against Gerald and helps expose him.

### Brenda
- Zion's half-sister.
- Mother of Chase.
- Supports Tory and helps her through the pregnancy secret.
- Her own trauma helps explain Zion's protectiveness toward the women in his family.

### Chase
- Brenda's daughter.
- Tory's friend from the school connection.
- Becomes very attached to Tory.
- Later is part of the family around Abby and the twins announcement.

### Zach
- Tory's café coworker and close friend.
- Helps with Tory's social and work life at Éclaire.

### Nana
- Tory's café boss and a major emotional figure.
- Dies during the pregnancy arc.

### Linda
- Household staff member who becomes close to Tory.
- Eventually agrees to be the baby's nanny.

## Relationship timeline
1. Flashback: Tory is 14 and Amelia is 20. The Wallsmans cannot clear their debt; the Davits demand marriage to one daughter.
2. Eight years later Tory is 22 and works at the café.
3. Tory and Zion meet at the café.
4. Tory sees Zion again through Chase at Amelia's school.
5. At the family engagement meeting, Zion changes his choice and selects Tory instead of Amelia.
6. Tory and Zion marry.
7. The marriage starts as a forced arrangement and gradually becomes a real romantic relationship.
8. The couple later faces the child/fatherhood conflict, the pregnancy secret, Nana's death, and the Gerald/Katherine conflict.
9. Tory leaves after misunderstanding Nataly's messages about their past baby.
10. Zion explains the truth, Tory sees the family home he built, and they reconcile.
11. Epilogue: three years later they have Abby and learn they are expecting twins.

## Adaptation rules
- The PDF is the authority. Do not invent missing events.
- Compression is allowed; invention is not.
- Preserve the order of causal events unless a visual edit only changes shot order without changing story order.
- Every episode must remain **≤60 seconds**.
- Six blocks are for storyboard/edit organization only; they are not six mandatory ten-second scenes.
- Never add filler to equalize block lengths.
- If a block contains several shots, keep the shots short enough for the total episode to remain ≤60 seconds.
- When later story development depends on a reveal, do not introduce the reveal earlier merely to make an episode easier to understand.
