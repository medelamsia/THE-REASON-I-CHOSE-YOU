# CANON

## Premise
Eight years after a family debt crisis, Tory Wallsman is living an ordinary life and working at a café. Zion Davit is a wealthy, arrogant CEO under family pressure to marry. Their first encounters begin before either knows the other's identity.

## Characters
### Tory Wallsman
- Age at main timeline: 22
- Brunette, natural appearance, practical clothing preference
- Works at a café; coffee is her specialty
- Wants to become a cosmetic chemist
- Sassy, blunt, funny, stubborn, emotionally guarded
- Very protective of her older sister Amelia

### Amelia Wallsman
- Tory's older sister
- Blonde, polished, organized, socially popular
- Teacher in the main timeline
- Once dreamed of becoming a model
- Her engagement/marriage arrangement becomes the story's central catalyst

### Zion Davit
- Age at main timeline: 29
- Wealthy CEO and heir within the Davit family
- Tall, green-eyed, controlled, arrogant, easily irritated
- Initially in a relationship with Nataly
- Resists the arranged marriage imposed by his family
- First notices Tory at the café before learning who she is

### Chase
- Young niece in Zion's family
- Friendly with Tory and helps connect Tory to Zion's family

### Zach
- Tory's café coworker
- Playful and annoying in a friendly way

### Nana
- Tory's café boss
- Warm, sharp-tongued, caring

### Gerald Davit
- Zion's father
- Uses family debt/business succession as leverage over Zion

### Nataly
- Zion's girlfriend before the forced marriage
- Her relationship with Zion creates ongoing emotional conflict

### Gustavo
- Charming man who first interacts with Tory at the wedding/reception and later helps her at the honeymoon hotel

### Linda
- Household staff member assigned to Tory
- Formal but caring; often provides exposition about the Davit household

## Timeline anchors
1. Flashback: Tory is 14 and Amelia is 20. The Wallsman family faces a large debt to the Davits. The Davits demand marriage to one of the sisters as part of the arrangement.
2. Eight years later: Tory is 22, working at a café.
3. Tory and Zion meet at the café.
4. Tory later encounters Zion again through Chase at Amelia's school.
5. At the arranged engagement gathering, Zion initially appears set to marry Amelia, then abruptly chooses Tory.
6. Tory and Zion marry.
7. The marriage remains emotionally hostile and complicated while Zion's prior relationship with Nataly continues to affect the story.

## Adaptation rules
- Keep Tory's humor, bluntness, and inner-conflict central.
- Keep Zion intimidating but show his internal contradiction rather than making him generically cruel.
- Build romance through tension, jealousy, forced proximity, and small behavioral changes rather than instant love.
- Preserve major reveals from the source; do not spoil future twists early.
- Every episode ends on a hook.
- Avoid adding unrelated characters, locations, or plot mechanics without tracking them here.
